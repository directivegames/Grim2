# Mood starting values

The short version is in [SKILL.md → Decide](../SKILL.md#decide). When the user did not say, pick the row for the game and apply it, one change per capture. These are starting values chosen for readability, not engine defaults. Engine defaults: [post-processing.md](post-processing.md), [lights-and-shadows.md](lights-and-shadows.md). Fog colour, grade tints, practical-light colours and the sky tint come from the `GAME.md` palette.

| Mood | Key light | Fill | Sky / environment | Tone map + exposure | Bloom | Grade | Fog |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bright arcade (platformer, party, puzzle, kart) | Directional, white-warm, intensity 2–3, high sun (50–65° elevation), `castShadow` on | Hemisphere, sky-blue over warm ground, 0.6–1.0 | `SkyNode` with low `turbidity` (≈2) and `skyBrightness` 0.3–0.45, or a procedural `SceneEnvironmentNode` gradient | ACESFilmic, exposure 0.4–0.6 with a `SkyNode`, 1.0 with a gradient | Off, or strength 0.3–0.5, threshold ≥ 1 | saturation 1.1–1.2, contrast 1.05 | None |
| Outdoor day (open world, racing, sports, survival) | Directional sun, intensity about 2–3.5, `drivesSky: true`, standard shadows sized to the area around the player (the pawn carries them), CSM off ([rule](lights-and-shadows.md#cascaded-shadows-default-off)). A `genesys-world-building` recipe or environment preset sets this row in one step | Hemisphere 0.3–0.6 (raise it, not the sun, when shaded slopes crush) | `SkyNode` with `skyBrightness` 0.3–0.45, `generateEnvironmentMap` and `environmentMapHdr` on, `environmentMapIntensity` 1; or a textured HDRI | ACESFilmic or AgX, exposure 0.4–0.6 with a `SkyNode` | Off or subtle (0.2–0.4) | Neutral; temperature +0.05 for warmth | Linear or exponential far haze matched to sky colour |
| Dark / horror / dusk / night (outdoor) | Keep the scene's directional at its existing intensity (about 4–6) and lower the sun toward the horizon (`drivesSky: true`) for a dusk sky; cool or blood-red tint | Hemisphere 2.5–3.5 (node above the origin, e.g. y = 1) **plus** ambient 1.0–1.5 in a muted violet or grey: the street must stay readable | The existing `SkyNode`, tuned: high `turbidity` (10–20), `rayleigh` 0.3–4 to taste, `cloudCoverage` 0.8, `showSunDisc: false` | ACESFilmic, exposure 1.0 | Only on emissive sources (windows, lamps, fire), threshold ≥ 0.9 | contrast 1.05–1.1, saturation 0.85–0.9; mood comes from colour, not from darkness | Exponential, **thin** (0.01–0.015), a muted mid colour near the horizon's (never near-black) |
| Space / sci-fi | Single hard directional "star" key, intensity 2–3, strong shadow | Very low ambient (≤ 0.1) plus a coloured rim from a second non-shadow light | Black or star HDRI / star skybox; no `SkyNode` | ACESFilmic, exposure 1.0 | On for engines, lasers, and emissive trims: strength 0.6–1.0, threshold ~0.9 | contrast 1.1; `shadowsTint` `0x2f8f9d` at 0.2, `highlightsTint` `0xffa25a` at 0.15 | None |
| Indoor / dungeon | Point and spot lights at sources (torches, lamps), shadows only on 1–3 key lights | Hemisphere or ambient 0.2–0.3, plus a `LightProbeGridNode` over the rooms; bake a lightmap once the static geometry is final | Environment map for reflections, background hidden by walls | ACESFilmic, exposure 1.0–1.2 | Subtle on flames | temperature +0.1 | Optional light exponential |
